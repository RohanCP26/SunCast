# Privacy Policy for SunCast

**Last Updated:** October 2026

## Data Collection
SunCast does not collect or store personal information. We only use:
- Your location (when you enter it manually)
- Weather data from public APIs (OpenWeatherMap, NWS)

## Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties.
