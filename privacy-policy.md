# Privacy Policy for SunCast

**Last Updated:** October 2026

## Data Collection

SunCast collects the information you add to an account and the information needed to show a forecast.

Account information:

- Username and display name
- Email address or phone number, or both
- A password, stored only as a hash
- Profile photo
- Posts, including the photo, caption, place name, and date you add
- Likes, ratings from 1 to 10, and comments
- Friend requests, friendships, blocks, and reports

Forecast information:

- The place you type, or the device location when you tap “Use my location”
- That location is sent with the forecast request. SunCast does not keep a history of those coordinates
- Weather comes from Open-Meteo
- Viewpoint search uses OpenStreetMap
- An optional sunset picture may be created by Replicate or Pollinations when those services are configured, or drawn on the SunCast server

Contacts:

- On a phone, Find friends in contacts asks for contact access
- The emails and phone numbers from that pass are sent so SunCast can suggest people who already have an account
- The contact list is not stored

A session token stays on your device for up to 30 days. Sign out removes it from that device and from the server.

## Data Sharing

SunCast does not sell your information.

Weather, map, and optional image providers receive the forecast request needed to answer it. A password reset code is sent to the email or phone on the account. A report may be emailed to the address below so it can be reviewed.

Photos you post can be seen by friends who are signed in. People who are not friends can see your name and username so they can send a request. They cannot see your posts.

## Account deletion

You can delete your account from your profile. That removes the profile, posts, photos, friendships, and session tokens.
